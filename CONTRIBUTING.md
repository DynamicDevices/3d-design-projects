# Contributing

Keep each project under `projects/<project-name>/` with a readable README and
machine-readable `project.json`. Use pull requests for changes after the initial
metadata bootstrap. Do not claim automated checks or review integrations exist
unless they are actually configured and working.

For designs, include editable source, intended units, exported-file identity,
printer/nozzle/material assumptions, print orientation and support requirements.
Distinguish concepts, calculated results and physically tested results. Record
fit/load limits and retain the accepted reference when making a new candidate.

Submit only material you have the right to distribute. Identify reused work,
its creator, source, exact licence and modifications before submission. Preserve
upstream notices. DD's non-commercial licence cannot narrow rights granted
directly by an upstream creator. Do not upload unverified dependencies.

Unless explicitly agreed otherwise, contributions to DD-licensed design or
documentation files are offered under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Contributors retain their
own copyright; this is not an assignment or an automatic grant to DD to offer
their contributions under a separate commercial licence.

General-purpose scripts and software need a separate explicit licensing decision
before inclusion. Never commit credentials, signed authority grants, private
photos, personal or infrastructure details, machine-local profiles or raw job
logs. Publish only reviewed, appropriately licensed evidence.

Use signed commits and include `Assisted-by: Briar` when applicable. Keep design
approval separate from manufacturing, structural and physical-test acceptance.
